FREEDAYS

Privacy policy

Freedays doesn't collect, store or sell your information. Your shifts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, which the developer can't see.

Last updated 29 September 2026

What the developer receives

Nothing. Freedays has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server holding your information and can't see your shifts, notes, events or who you share with.

Information kept on your iPhone

Your shift types, patterns, changed days, notes, events and settings are saved on your iPhone. Home Screen widgets read a copy stored on the same iPhone.

iCloud

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is on (it is unless you switch it off in Settings › Backup & restore), your Freedays data is stored in your private iCloud storage, run by Apple, so it appears on your other devices and on a new iPhone. Only you can access it. Apple Calendar settings aren't synced.

Sharing your shifts

When you share, a copy of your shift types, pattern and changed days is stored in your iCloud and made available to the people you invite. They also see any notes and events you've chosen to share, but never the notes inside an event. People you share with can view but not change anything. You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time in Settings › Sharing › Manage sharing.

Shifts other people share with you are stored in your iCloud and on your iPhone so they're available offline.

Sharing and sync use Apple's iCloud and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions Freedays may ask for

  • Calendars, only if you turn on Apple Calendar features: to write your shifts to a calendar called My Shifts, and to show events from calendars you choose. Calendar information stays on your iPhone and in your own calendars.
  • Camera, only when you scan a printed shift pattern. Scanned pages and any screenshots or photos you choose are read entirely on your iPhone. They aren't saved by Freedays or uploaded anywhere.

Connections Freedays makes

  • UK bank holidays are downloaded from GOV.UK.
  • Update checks read a small version file from GitHub, to tell TestFlight testers about new builds.

Neither request includes any of your information. Like any website, those services see your device's internet address when the file is downloaded.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer doesn't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

Freedays is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Removing your information

Deleting Freedays removes everything stored on your iPhone. To remove your synced and shared data from iCloud, go to the iPhone Settings app › your name › iCloud › Storage, choose Freedays and delete its data. Because the developer holds nothing, there's nothing further to request, but you're welcome to ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
